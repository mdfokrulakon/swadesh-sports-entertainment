One name behind three very different grounds

Swadesh Sports & Entertainment is the sport and entertainment arm of Swadesh Group. Under it sit three properties built for different kinds of play — a purpose‑built cricket venue at Shornali Abashon, a multi‑sport club at Sunvalley Abashon, and Chinese Sports Club, a community built around fitness and friendly competition.

Cricket, and only cricket

Dhaka's ground built for cricket

Swadesh Arena is a single-purpose cricket venue — no shared bookings, no other codes on the pitch. Every part of the ground, from the turf to the lighting rig, is set up around a match day.

01

Match-standard turf

A maintained pitch and outfield built to hold up across a full playing season.

02

Seating for thousands

Tiered spectator seating designed so every seat keeps a clear line to the pitch.

03

VIP lounges

Covered hospitality seating for sponsors, officials and guests on match days.

04

Food & beverage

On-site outlets so spectators don't have to leave the stands between overs.

05

Floodlights & sound

Lighting and PA built for day and evening fixtures alike.

06

On-site parking

Direct access from the 300ft Purbachal Express Highway with dedicated parking.
